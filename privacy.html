Your account, at a glance

You can browse without an account. Google sign-in identifies you; your profile adds your name and phone number. When ordering is available, checkout collects delivery details and sends order updates. Creating an account does not subscribe you to marketing emails.

1. Who we are

Anurag Apparel operates this website for its apparel store at Malviya Marg, Hazaribagh, Jharkhand, India. This policy covers information handled through this website, customer accounts and online orders when available.

2. Information we collect

  • Google sign-in information. When you choose Google sign-in, Google provides identity information such as your name, email address, account identifier and, if available, profile-picture URL. Our authentication provider records your identity and sign-in session. We do not receive your Google password or request access to your Gmail, Drive or contacts.
  • Customer profile. We store the full name and phone number you provide, your customer-account identifier, and profile creation and update times. Your phone number is supplied by you and is not verified by an SMS code.
  • Order and delivery details. When you order, we store your selected items, sizes and colours, quantities, prices, delivery fee, recipient name, email, phone number, address, PIN code, delivery map point, notes, delivery slot, cash-payment status and order/status history. The delivery address is kept as an order snapshot; later profile edits do not change it. We do not collect card or online-payment details for cash-on-delivery orders.
  • Location. Checkout requires a map point so we can check the delivery area and find your address. You can choose it manually or allow the browser to provide your location after selecting the location button. We do not continuously track your location. The submitted point is supplied by you and may not be independently verified.
  • Delivery verification. Some orders require a four-digit acceptance code. We keep an encrypted code record and a protected verifier, together with failed-attempt counts and lock times. The code is shown through the signed-in customer's order screen; it is not included in order emails or displayed in staff order records.
  • Browsing and interaction information. The storefront records page visits and interactions, such as viewing products or clicking contact, directions and social links. These records can include browser and session identifiers, the page and referring URL, browser details, language, time zone, screen dimensions and information about the product or link you selected. These analytics use browser identifiers rather than your Google email address.
  • Messages and store communications. If you contact us or separately request store emails, we may handle your contact details and the information you choose to provide.

3. How we use information

We use account information to identify you, keep you signed in, maintain your customer profile and respond to account or shopping enquiries. Order information is used to reserve stock, check delivery eligibility, schedule and fulfil your order, contact the recipient, verify delivery, record cash collection and resolve order issues. We send transactional emails when an order is booked, delivered or cancelled.

Authorised store staff can view the customer and delivery information needed to manage accounts and orders. Submitted addresses and map points appear in their delivery dashboard. Browsing information helps us improve the website and investigate misuse or technical problems.

Google account information is used for sign-in and the account features described here. We do not sell it or use it for personalised advertising. Signing in does not add you to our marketing contact list.

The store can pause online ordering while keeping the catalogue and existing order history available. Cash on delivery is the website's payment method when online ordering is enabled.

4. Services and sharing

We use service providers to operate the website. Information is handled as needed to provide their services:

  • Google handles Google sign-in. Embedded Google Maps, reviewer images and links to Google services may also connect your browser to Google.
  • Supabase provides authentication, stores customer profiles, orders, delivery information and browsing analytics, runs server operations and serves catalogue media.
  • OpenStreetMap supplies map tiles for checkout and staff delivery maps. Map requests can reveal technical information and the area being viewed; the delivery address remains in our order system. Library files are delivered by a content provider.
  • Website hosting and content providers deliver the site, libraries and images. These include Vercel, jsDelivr, unpkg, Tailwind's CDN and, where used, Unsplash. Requests to these services may expose technical information such as your IP address, browser details and referrer, according to the service and browser settings.
  • Resend delivers personalised booking, delivery and cancellation emails containing order details, and separately arranged marketing emails. Creating a Google account or placing an order does not enrol you in marketing.

Providers may process information in countries other than where you live. You can read more about their privacy practices in the policies published by Google, Supabase, Vercel, Resend and OpenStreetMap Foundation.

We may disclose information where required by law or where reasonably necessary to investigate abuse, protect people or address security incidents. Public Google reviews displayed on the site may contain the reviewer's published name, photo and review text. External social and map links lead to services with their own policies.

5. Browser storage and security

Customer sign-in uses browser storage to remember your authentication session. Cart selections are saved locally for the current customer or guest, and checkout retry identifiers help prevent duplicate bookings after a connection failure. We do not persist the full delivery address or plaintext delivery code in cart storage. The storefront also stores a browser identifier locally and a separate identifier for the browsing session. Local storage can remain until you clear site data; session storage normally lasts for the current tab session.

You can sign out through My account and clear stored site data in your browser settings. Clearing site data may sign you out and reset the local identifiers; it does not itself delete records already stored by the website. Blocking browser storage may prevent sign-in from working.

We use authenticated customer access controls, staff session checks and encrypted HTTPS connections. Delivery codes are encrypted in the application database with a key held by the server, and incorrect verification attempts are limited. A person controlling server code and keys can change those controls. Hosting and authentication services may maintain technical and security logs. No method of online storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

6. How long we keep information

We keep account and profile information while needed to provide your account or address a legitimate support, security or legal requirement. You can request account and profile deletion using the contact details below.

Active orders are kept for fulfilment. For delivered and cancelled orders, the store can configure separate history periods for customers and staff, measured from delivery or cancellation. A history item may disappear from your account while remaining available to staff for the longer configured period. When automatic retention is enabled, the shared order is permanently deleted from the application database after both periods have expired. A period set to no expiry prevents automatic permanent deletion; contact us about the current retention settings or a deletion request.

Staff may permanently delete a completed or cancelled order earlier. This removes it for both audiences, including its line items, status events, code records and queued email records. Delivery-code records are also removed when an order is delivered or cancelled. Application deletion does not recall already-sent emails or immediately remove provider backups or technical logs; those follow their own retention schedules.

The website is configured to purge browsing analytics older than 90 days. Scheduled processing, backups and provider logs may follow different deletion times. Where information must be retained for a legal obligation or an unresolved issue, we may retain the relevant information for that purpose.

7. Your choices and requests

You can update your name and phone number through My account. Your sign-in email is managed through Google. Contact the store promptly if an order address or other delivery information is incorrect. You can also request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account/order information, or raise a privacy concern. We may need to verify that you own the account before responding.

You can review or remove this app's Google access in your Google Account connections. Removing Google access does not automatically delete your Anurag Apparel profile; contact us to request its deletion. You can stop using the account and browse the public collection without signing in.

If you separately receive marketing emails, you can ask us to stop them by emailing the store. Account creation alone does not give marketing consent.

8. Children's information

Customer accounts are intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children. If you believe a child has supplied personal information through an account, please contact us so we can review it and arrange appropriate removal.

9. Policy updates

We may update this policy as the website or its data practices change. The revised policy will appear on this page with an updated date. Please check it when you use a new account or shopping feature.

10. Contact us about privacy

For account deletion, data requests or privacy concerns, contact Anurag Apparel and describe your request. Please use the email linked to your customer account where possible.
